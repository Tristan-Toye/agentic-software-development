# Value

- The owner sees what changes, in the same picture as what stays. Removed items stay visible in `changes` mode.
- The owner can switch to `to-be` to see the planned product, and to `as-is` to see today.
- The counts give a short list to tick through ([review-ux-and-feedback](../../research/synthesis/review-ux-and-feedback.md), section 3).
- The validation rule detects a stale plan before the owner spends time on it ([Q8](../../research/synthesis/q8-staleness-and-living-map.md)).

Not claimed: that colour marks alone are enough. Two mark channels are required for colour-blind users ([review-ux-and-feedback](../../research/synthesis/review-ux-and-feedback.md), section 3).
